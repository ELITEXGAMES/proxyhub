1. Scope & Applicability

This Policy applies comprehensively to all individuals, developers, automated programs, API clients, testing scripts, and autonomous AI agents (including OpenClaw, Claude Code, Continue, Cursor) that interact with or send network requests to this Antigravity Proxy deployment.

By generating API credentials, connecting Google accounts or cookies, or transmitting payloads through /v1/chat/completions, /v1/messages, or media endpoints, you warrant that your actions strictly conform to this Policy.

2. Zero Tolerance Violations

The Operator maintains an absolute ZERO-TOLERANCE policy regarding the following offenses. Any detected occurrence will result in immediate termination, permanent blacklisting, and reporting to legal authorities without prior notice:

🚫 Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) & Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE)

Generating, soliciting, enhancing, altering, or transmitting any representation of sexual abuse, exploitation, or grooming involving minors. Fully prohibited under Section 67B of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012.

🚫 Terrorism, Violent Extremism & Insurgency

Advocating, planning, facilitating, or promoting acts of terrorism, violence against religious, ethnic, or national groups, or threats to national security and public order under the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA) and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.

🚫 Suicide & Self-Harm Encouragement

Generating instructions, encouragement, or methods promoting self-harm, suicide, or eating disorders.

🚫 Weaponization & Chemical/Biological Weapons

Generating synthesis pathways, weaponization methodologies, or dispersion instructions for chemical, biological, radiological, or nuclear (CBRN) agents.

3. Category-Specific Prohibitions

You agree NOT to use the Service or its generated completions for any of the following activities:

A. Fraud, Deception, Impersonation & Scams

  • Creating deceptive synthetic identities, phishing lures, fake bank notices, government spoofing documents, or advance-fee scam templates.
  • Impersonating living individuals, public officials, law enforcement personnel, or corporations without authorization (Section 66D, IT Act 2000).
  • Generating misleading financial advice, market manipulation schemes, fraudulent securities prospectuses, or deceptive cryptocurrency promotions.

B. Defamation, Harassment & Non-Consensual Imagery

  • Conducting targeted cyber-bullying, stalking, dox attacks, or intimidation campaigns against any individual.
  • Generating non-consensual deepfakes, intimate imagery, or fabricated explicit media depicting identifiable living persons (Section 66E and 67A, IT Act 2000).
  • Generating defamatory statements calculated to injure the reputation or livelihood of any person or legal entity.

C. Privacy Violations & Unauthorized Surveillance

  • Processing, extracting, or aggregating sensitive personal data without explicit legal consent under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • Attempting to reconstruct personal identifying numbers (such as Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID, passport details, or credit card CVVs).

4. Cyber Security & Unauthorized Access

Under Sections 43, 66, and 66F of the Information Technology Act, 2000, you are strictly prohibited from utilizing the Service to:

  • Malware & Exploits: Write, refine, test, or distribute malicious code, trojan horses, ransomware, rootkits, worm propagation routines, or zero-day exploit payloads.
  • Network Attacks: Execute unauthorized automated vulnerability scans, port knocking, credential stuffing, dictionary attacks, or penetration testing against any server without explicit written permission from the owner.
  • System Tampering: Attempting to penetrate the host system running Antigravity Proxy, access arbitrary local files outside public/media/, or execute privilege escalation attacks.

5. Bypassing Third-Party Safety Guardrails & Provider Policies

Antigravity Proxy functions as a protocol bridge, not a tool for defeating artificial intelligence safeguards:

  • No Jailbreaking / Adversarial Injections: You shall not use prompt injection, adversarial jailbreaking strings, or multi-turn exploits designed to force upstream models (Google Gemini, NVIDIA NIM) to violate their fundamental safety filters or terms.
  • Respect Upstream Terms: You must maintain valid rights, subscriptions, or API access for all upstream accounts connected to the proxy. Using stolen accounts, unauthorized corporate cookies, or compromised API tokens is strictly prohibited.
  • No Commercial Subversion: You shall not use the proxy to bypass geographic tiering or commercial resale restrictions imposed by upstream model vendors.

6. Infrastructure, Resource Abuse & Rate Limit Evasion

To ensure fair operational access and avoid resource exhaustion on the local host and upstream APIs:

  • Respect Rate Limits: You must respect configured sliding window rate limits (120 RPM default). Generating distributed requests from multiple proxies or spoofing client IP headers (X-Forwarded-For) to bypass limits is considered malicious behavior.
  • No Denial of Service: Flooding the server with excessive concurrent streaming requests, multi-gigabyte payloads, or malicious infinite token loops is strictly forbidden.
  • Storage Pruning: If you utilize local media generation (/v1/images/generations or /v1/videos/generations), you must maintain reasonable storage quotas on the local disk.

7. Investigation, Enforcement & Penalties

The Operator enforces this Policy through automated safeguards and administrative oversight:

  • Automated Rejection: The server employs parameter validation, rate limiting, and size boundaries to reject abusive traffic programmatically.
  • Administrative Review: In the event of system instability, crash reports, or abuse alerts, the Operator may review aggregate metadata (status codes, request frequency, model usage).
  • Penalties for Violation: The Operator reserves the right to take any of the following actions immediately without prior notice or liability:
    1. Instant revocation and deletion of offending Proxy API keys.
    2. Permanent firewall blacklisting of violating IP subnets.
    3. Disconnection and invalidation of connected upstream session cookies.
    4. Reporting the violation to upstream model vendors and appropriate law enforcement bodies.

8. Mandatory Statutory Reporting & Law Enforcement Cooperation

In accordance with the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 and the directions issued by the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) under Section 70B(6) of the IT Act, 2000:

The Operator will cooperate fully with lawful orders, warrants, and directives issued by competent law enforcement agencies, cyber crime investigation cells, and judicial courts in India. When required by applicable statutory orders, relevant metadata and connection logs will be preserved and produced to investigating officers.
